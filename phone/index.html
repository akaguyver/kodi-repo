Cookie extension for Firefox on Android

Sortovia's YouTube downloads need your browser's cookies. This is cookies.txt 1.0, the signed copy from addons.mozilla.org, marked for Firefox for Android 121 or newer. It's under the GPL-3.0 licence, and its source code is on GitHub.

  1. Press and hold this link, then choose Download link. Tapping it normally may be blocked.
    cookies-txt.xpi (87 KB)
  2. In Firefox, go to Settings → About Firefox and tap the Firefox logo 5 times.
  3. Go back to Settings, choose Install extension from file, and pick the file from your Download folder.

The rest of the steps are in Sortovia, under Help & settings → Read the guide.
