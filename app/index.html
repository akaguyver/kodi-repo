Kodi Sorter companion

Android app that shares your phone's Download folder with Kodi Sorter on your Xbox (or any Kodi) over the phone's hotspot. No Termux needed.

Download the app (version 0.1.1)

  1. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  2. Open Kodi Sorter companion and tap through Phone settings.
  3. Turn on the Mobile hotspot, connect the Xbox, tap Start sharing.
  4. In Kodi Sorter on the Xbox choose My phone over Wi-Fi and type the password the app shows.
