Kodi Sorter companion

Android app that shares your phone's Download folder with Kodi Sorter on your Xbox (or any Kodi) over the phone's hotspot. No Termux needed.

Download the app (version 0.1.2)

  1. Allow installing it (it isn't from the Play Store). Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps > your browser (or My Files) > Allow. Samsung phones: also turn off Auto Blocker (Settings > Security and privacy > Auto Blocker) - you can turn it back on after installing. If Play Protect warns, choose More details > Install anyway.
  2. Download the app above and open the downloaded file.
  3. Open Kodi Sorter companion and allow the Phone settings first (all files access, notifications, run in the background).
  4. Turn on the Mobile hotspot, connect the Xbox, tap Start sharing.
  5. In Kodi Sorter on the Xbox choose My phone over Wi-Fi and type the password the app shows.

Used xbox-share in Termux before? Keep its password: in Termux type xbox-share password, then in the app tap Change password and type it.
