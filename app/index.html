Sortovia companion

Android app that lets Sortovia in Kodi import files from your phone over Wi-Fi or the phone's hotspot. No Termux needed.

Download the app (version 0.1.7)

  1. Allow installing it (it isn't from the Play Store). Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps > your browser (or My Files) > Allow. Samsung phones: also turn off Auto Blocker (Settings > Security and privacy > Auto Blocker) - you can turn it back on after installing. If Play Protect warns, choose More details > Install anyway.
  2. Download the app above and open the downloaded file.
  3. Open Sortovia companion and allow the Phone settings first (all files access, notifications, run in the background).
  4. Put your Kodi device on the phone's Mobile hotspot or the same Wi-Fi, then tap Start sharing.
  5. In Sortovia choose My phone over Wi-Fi and type the password the app shows.

Used kodi-share in Termux before? Keep its password: in Termux type kodi-share password, then in the app tap Change password and type it.
